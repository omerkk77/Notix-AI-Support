How it works

From your material to a real learning path.

Not a generic template. Every course is planned from what you upload, at your level, in your language.

1

Add your material

Drop in a file, paste a link, record a lecture, snap a page, or just type a topic.

2

Get a course path

Notix plans bite-sized lessons, checkpoints and a final, in the order that makes sense.

3

Learn, then practice

Read one lesson at a time, then answer exercises with instant feedback. Recalling beats re-reading.

Everything in one place

One upload. Every way to study it.

Each course comes with its own toolkit, generated from the same source, so everything stays consistent.

Course path

A clear outline of what to learn and in which order, with lessons that teach first and test after.

Notes

Structured, editable study notes with key points. Ask the AI to simplify, expand or rewrite any section.

Chat with your document

Ask questions and get answers grounded in your own material, with follow-up suggestions.

Quizzes

Test yourself on any document and see exactly where you stand before the exam.

Flashcards

Swipe through terms and definitions generated from your notes. Review the ones you miss.

Podcast

Turn a document into an audio episode and keep learning on the bus, at the gym or on a walk.
