Notix AI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026
Effective Date: April 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Notix AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Notix AI (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the App immediately.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Email address and password when you create an account (managed via Supabase Auth).
  • Profile Information: Name or display name you choose to provide during onboarding.
  • Content You Upload: Documents (PDF, DOCX, PPT), text, audio recordings, scanned images, and YouTube links you submit for analysis.
  • Notes and Edits: Any notes, renames, or annotations you add to your analyses.
  • Communications: Messages you send through AI chat sessions within the App.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features you use, analysis types performed, frequency of use, and interaction patterns.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, unique identifiers, language, and locale settings.
  • Analytics Data: Performance data and crash reports collected via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Subscription Data: Status and entitlements managed via RevenueCat. We do not store raw payment info.

2.3 Device Permissions We Request

Permission Purpose
Microphone To record audio for voice and lecture analysis.
Camera To scan physical documents using OCR.
Photo Library To select images of documents for analysis.
Speech Recognition To enable voice input for searches and messages.
Notifications To notify you when AI analysis is complete.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service: Analyze documents, videos, and text using AI models.
  • Generate Study Materials: Create flashcards, quizzes, and interview sessions.
  • Manage Subscriptions: Verify status and unlock features via RevenueCat.
  • Security: Monitor for fraudulent or illegal activity.
Note: We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your uploaded content to train our AI models.

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Service Providers

Provider Purpose
Supabase Database storage, authentication, edge functions.
Google (Gemini/Firebase) AI analysis processing, analytics.
RevenueCat Subscription and in-app purchase management.

5. Data Storage and Security

Storage: Data is stored securely in Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure). We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) for all data transmission. Access is protected by row-level security policies.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data at any time through the App settings. We comply with GDPR (EEA/UK) and CCPA (California) requirements regarding your personal information.
